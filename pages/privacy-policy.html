Data Protection & Privacy Commitment

SunCapital is committed to protecting the privacy of property owners, drone LIDAR telemetry, and smart meter energy consumption data.

1. Drone LIDAR & Roof Spatial Data Handling

Drone sensors collect high-resolution 3D point cloud telemetry for the explicit purpose of rendering rooftop solar CAD layout options. LIDAR spatial data is stored on encrypted cloud servers (AES-256) and is never sold, shared, or licensed to third-party advertising entities.

2. Smart Energy Meter & Inverter Telemetry

Our mobile application receives real-time kilowatt-hour generation and consumption metrics from your solar inverter. This telemetry is transmitted via TLS 1.3 encryption solely to render dashboard analytics and automated diagnostic alerts.

3. Federal Tax Subsidy & Municipal Filing Data

Personal information provided for 30% federal tax credit assistance or net metering 3.0 approval is transmitted exclusively to authorized municipal building departments and official public utility commissions.

4. GDPR & CCPA Consumer Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), property owners maintain full rights to request data deletion, export their solar telemetry archives, or opt out of telemetry processing.
