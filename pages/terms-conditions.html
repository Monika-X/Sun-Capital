Service Terms & Installation Agreements

1. 25-Year Equipment & Craftsmanship Warranty

SunCapital warrants that all installed monocrystalline photovoltaic modules shall maintain at least 85% of their initial rated power generation capacity after 25 years of continuous operation. All roof mounting flashing carries a 10-year weatherproof seal warranty.

2. Property Access & Structural Prerequisites

The property owner agrees to grant SunCapital certified engineers safe physical access to the rooftop, main electrical breaker panel, and grounds during agreed installation hours. Roof structural framing must be certified sound via drone CAD scan.

3. Federal Tax Subsidy & Utility Net Metering Disclaimer

While SunCapital provides concierge administrative support for claiming the 30% Federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC) and net metering 3.0 approval, final tax credit eligibility remains subject to IRS guidelines and individual tax counsel.

Yield Protection

Annual Power Generation Guarantee

If your SunCapital solar array fails to produce at least 90% of projected annual kilowatt-hours due to equipment degradation, SunCapital will reimburse the financial bill difference directly.

Equipment Maintenance & Inverter Replacement

Smart solar inverters are rated for a 15-year operational lifespan. Routine solar panel cleaning and thermal sensor inspections are covered under SunCapital Concierge SLA maintenance plans.
